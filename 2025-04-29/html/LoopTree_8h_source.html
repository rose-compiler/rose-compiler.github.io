17 /* QY: The LoopTree data structure is a tree structure built as a wrapper
18 of the real AST. The AST nodes are treated differently depending on whether
19 the node represents a loop. There are two main different kinds of LoopTree
20 nodes: LoopNode and StmtNode. All the non-loop ASTs are treated as atomic
21 stmts (Stmt nodes) which are never modified. All the loops are reconstructed
22 with internal symbolic information. The tree structure will be modified
23 (loop optimizations). After transformation, calling CodeGen will re-construct
24 the transformed AST. The goal is to support loop optimization more easily. */
