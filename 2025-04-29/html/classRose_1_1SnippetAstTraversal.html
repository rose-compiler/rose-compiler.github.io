Detailed Description

Java-aware AST traversal. This is a pre/post depth-first traversal that is aware of certain Java attributes and follows them even when AstSimpleProcessing would not follow them. The functor should take two arguments: SgNode*, and AstSimpleProcessing::Order (the constant preorder or postorder depending on whether the call is before or after the children are traversed). Attributes are considered to be the first children of a node.
