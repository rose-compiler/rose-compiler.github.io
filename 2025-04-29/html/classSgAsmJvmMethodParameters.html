Detailed Description

JVM MethodParameters attribute.

The MethodParameters attribute is a variable-length attribute in the attributes table of a method_info structure (4.6). A MethodParameters attribute records information about the formal parameters of a method, such as their names. See section 4.7.24 of the JVM specification.
